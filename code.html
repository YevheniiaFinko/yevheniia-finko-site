[ ABOUT & PHILOSOPHY ]

I bring 15 years of operating experience across hospitality, furniture production and wellness. Those businesses taught me the same thing: growth becomes expensive when everything important lives in the founder’s head.

Today I run NEWYOU, a Ukrainian wellness brand. I started building systems because I wanted clearer numbers, fewer manual handoffs and less time spent chasing routine work.

Since December 2025, I have been building and testing AI assistants inside my own operations across planning, finance, analytics, content and follow-up. That practical experience shapes how I work with other founders: first understand the process, then decide whether AI belongs in it.

I work at the intersection of business operations, process design and practical AI implementation. I do not use AI for the sake of it, but add it where it makes the business calmer, clearer and easier to run.
