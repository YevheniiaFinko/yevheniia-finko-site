Privacy policy

Website yevheniia-finko-site.vercel.app · last updated: 2 November 2026

Who is responsible for your data

Yevheniia Finko, Barcelona, Spain. Contact for any privacy question: euheniia.finko@gmail.com.

What data I collect

  • Your message: if you use the contact form, your name, email, the description of your task and, if you choose to fill it in, the budget range you have in mind. Please do not put passwords, health data or other sensitive details in the message.
  • Your booking: if you book a call through Zcal, the details you enter there are processed by Zcal for the booking.
  • Email correspondence: if you write to me by email, I keep that correspondence.
  • Technical data (IP address, browser type): processed by the hosting provider to deliver the website.

I do not collect payment data and I do not use advertising cookies. To see how many people visit the site and where they come from, I use Vercel Web Analytics: it does not use cookies and does not identify you personally (it counts page views, the referring site, country and device type, and clicks on the booking button).

Why and on what basis

  • To read your message and reply to it. Basis: your consent (GDPR Art. 6.1.a), which you give by ticking the box in the form, and steps you ask for before a possible agreement (Art. 6.1.b).
  • To keep a record of our conversation so I can follow up. Basis: my legitimate interest in running my business (Art. 6.1.f).

I may use an AI assistant to sort incoming messages and to prepare a draft reply. Nothing is sent automatically: I read every draft and send the reply myself. No decision about you is made by an automated system.

Who else processes your data

  • Web3Forms, which delivers the contact form message to my inbox.
  • Google (Gmail), where I receive and store messages and drafts.
  • Anthropic (Claude), the AI assistant mentioned above, which sees the text of a message when it helps me sort it or draft a reply.
  • Zcal, for booking calls.
  • Vercel, to host the website and provide cookieless analytics.
  • Google Fonts and a styling library are loaded from third-party servers when you open the site, so those providers can see your IP address.

These providers may be located outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Transfers rely on the safeguards in their data processing terms, such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the EU standard contractual clauses.

I do not sell your data.

How long I keep it

Messages that do not lead to a working relationship: up to 12 months after the last contact, then deleted. If we work together, I keep what is needed for the work and for my legal obligations. You can ask me to delete your data earlier at any time.

Your rights

You can ask me to access, correct or delete your data, restrict or object to its processing, receive it in a portable format, and withdraw your consent at any time. Write to the contact above. You also have the right to complain to the Spanish data protection authority, the AEPD (aepd.es), or to the authority in your own country.

Children

This website is not meant for people under 16.

Changes

If I change this policy, the new version appears on this page with a new date.
